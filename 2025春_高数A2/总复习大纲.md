 ### 8.2 点乘和叉乘

> - **点乘**
> $
> \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = |\mathbf{u}|  |\mathbf{v}|  \cos\theta
> $
> 
> - **叉乘**
> $
> \mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v} = 
> \begin{vmatrix}
> \mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\
> u_1 & u_2 & u_3 \\
> v_1 & v_2 & v_3
> \end{vmatrix}
> $
> 几何意义：面积
>
> - **重要性质**
> 模长：$|\mathbf{u}|^2 = \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{u}$
> 投影：$\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{\hat{n}} = |\mathbf{u}|  \cos\theta$

 ### 8.3 平面方程

## 第九章: 多元微分

### 9.1 基本概念

> - 内点, 外点, 边界点.

### 9.2 偏导数

> **一阶偏导数**  
>
> - $f$ 对 $x$ 的偏导数: $\displaystyle\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}$
>
> - $f$ 对 $y$ 的偏导数: $\displaystyle\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}$
>
> **二阶偏导数**  
>
> - $f$ 对 $x$ 的二阶偏导： $\displaystyle\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x^2} = f_{xx} = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\right)$
> - $f$ 对 $y$ 的二阶偏导： $\displaystyle\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial y^2} = f_{yy} = \frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\right)$
>
> - 先对 $x$ 后对 $y$： $\displaystyle\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial y \partial x} = f_{xy} = \frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\right)$ 
> - 先对 $y$ 后对 $x$： $\displaystyle\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x \partial y} = f_{yx} = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\right)$ 

### 9.3  全微分

> $
> df = \displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{\partial f}{\partial x} dx + \displaystyle\frac{\partial f}{\partial y} dy
> $

### 9.4 多元微分链式法则

> **二元函数情形**
> 
> $
> \displaystyle\frac{\partial f}{\partial u} = \displaystyle\frac{\partial f}{\partial x} \displaystyle\frac{\partial x}{\partial u} + \displaystyle\frac{\partial f}{\partial y} \displaystyle\frac{\partial y}{\partial u}
> $
> 
> $
> \displaystyle\frac{\partial f}{\partial v} = \displaystyle\frac{\partial f}{\partial x} \displaystyle\frac{\partial x}{\partial v} + \displaystyle\frac{\partial f}{\partial y} \displaystyle\frac{\partial y}{\partial v}
> $
>


### 9.5  隐函数求导

> 给定隐函数方程组：
> $
> \begin{cases} 
> F(x, y, u, v) = 0 \\ 
> G(x, y, u, v) = 0 
> \end{cases}
> $
> 其中 $u = u(x,y)$, $v = v(x,y)$ 是由方程组确定的隐函数。
>
> **(1) 对 $x$ 求导**
> $
> \displaystyle\begin{cases}
> \displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{\partial F}{\partial x} + \displaystyle\frac{\partial F}{\partial u}\displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} + \displaystyle\frac{\partial F}{\partial v}\displaystyle\frac{\partial v}{\partial x} = 0 \\
> \displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{\partial G}{\partial x} + \displaystyle\frac{\partial G}{\partial u}\displaystyle\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} + \displaystyle\frac{\partial G}{\partial v}\displaystyle\frac{\partial v}{\partial x} = 0
> \end{cases}
> $
>
> **(2) 对 $y$ 求导**
> $
> \displaystyle\begin{cases}
> \displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{\partial F}{\partial y} + \displaystyle\frac{\partial F}{\partial u}\displaystyle\frac{\partial u}{\partial y} + \displaystyle\frac{\partial F}{\partial v}\displaystyle\frac{\partial v}{\partial y} = 0 \\
> \displaystyle\displaystyle\frac{\partial G}{\partial y} + \displaystyle\frac{\partial G}{\partial u}\displaystyle\frac{\partial u}{\partial y} + \displaystyle\frac{\partial G}{\partial v}\displaystyle\frac{\partial v}{\partial y} = 0
> \end{cases}
> $

### 9.7 方向导数与梯度
> - **梯度:** 梯度是一个向量，指向函数增长最快的方向
> $\displaystyle\nabla f = \left( \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}, \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}, \frac{\partial f}{\partial z} \right)^T
> $.
> 
> - **方向导数**
> $\displaystyle\frac{\partial f}{\partial \mathbf{\ell}} = \nabla f \cdot \mathbf{\ell} = \|\nabla f\| \cos\theta
> $（其中 $\theta$ 是梯度与方向 $\mathbf{\ell}$ 的夹角）.

###  9.8 极值与条件极值

> - 临界点条件：
> $\displaystyle\nabla f(\mathbf{x}_0) = \mathbf{0} \quad \text{即} \quad \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_i}(\mathbf{x}_0) = 0
> $
>
> - 海森矩阵：
>   $
>   \begin{bmatrix}
>   \displaystyle\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x^2} & \displaystyle\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x \partial y} \\
>   \displaystyle\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial y \partial x} & \displaystyle\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial y^2}
>   \end{bmatrix}
>   $
>   - 特征值全为正：极小值
>   - 特征值全为负：极大值
>   - 混合特征值：无法判断

> - 条件极值-**拉格朗日乘子法**：
>   - 约束条件：$\phi(x,y) = c$
>   - 拉格朗日函数：
>     $$
>     \mathcal{F}(x, y, \lambda) = f(x, y) + \lambda (\phi(x, y) - c)
>     $$
>   - 极值条件：
>     $$
>     \displaystyle\frac{\partial \mathcal{F}}{\partial x} = 0, \quad \displaystyle\frac{\partial \mathcal{F}}{\partial y} = 0, \quad \displaystyle\frac{\partial \mathcal{F}}{\partial \lambda} = 0
>     $$
